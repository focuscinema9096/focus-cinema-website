Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our production standards, equipment, shoot licensing, and booking process.

What video production services does Focus Cinema specialize in? +

Focus Cinema is a full-service creative agency specializing in luxury real estate films, licensed drone aerial cinematography, hospitality and resort coverage, commercial advertising, architectural still photography, and high-retention social media reels.

Where are you based and do you travel for shoots across India? +

We are founded and based in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), India. Our crew regularly travels for landmark productions across India, including Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Udaipur, Delhi NCR, Lucknow, Jaipur, Goa, and Bangalore.

Are your drone operations licensed and DGCA compliant? +

Yes. All aerial drone operations are conducted by certified pilots adhering strictly to DGCA regulations and airspace permissions. We utilize professional DJI camera drones equipped with high-dynamic-range sensors for cinema-grade 4K aerials.

What is the expected turnaround time for video delivery? +

Most commercial property and drone walkthrough films are edited, graded, and delivered within 3 to 7 working days after shoot completion, complete with social cut-downs and high-resolution stills.

How do we book a shoot or request a production quote? +

Simply click Start a project, chat directly with us on WhatsApp (+91 90960 12144), or email focuscinemaproduction@gmail.com. We review your vision and deliver a tailored treatment and quote within 24 hours.
